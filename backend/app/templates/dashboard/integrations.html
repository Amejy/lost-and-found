Examples

Quick requests

List lost items 
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" {{ base_url }}/api/v1/lost-items
Create a found item 
curl -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \\
  -d '{"title":"Phone","description":"Black iPhone","category":"Electronics","location":"Library","date_found":"2026-07-26"}' \\
  {{ base_url }}/api/v1/found-items
Review a claim 
curl -X PATCH -H "Content-Type: application/json" -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \\
  -d '{"decision":"approve","admin_notes":"Matches serial number"}' \\
  {{ base_url }}/api/v1/claims/123/review
